03 — Capabilities

What makes a figure read as a person.

  1. 01

    Custom knowledge

    Point it at your own material and it answers from that, not from the open internet. Change a price and it changes that afternoon.

  2. 02

    Real-time responses

    No scripted branch tree. The reply is generated against what it actually retrieved, so an unusual question gets a real answer rather than a deflection.

  3. 03

    AI personality

    A persona you define — name, tone, boundaries — so the same platform greets for a hotel at nine and a members’ club at midnight.

  4. 04

    Natural speech

    Synthesis tuned per deployment, with phoneme timing so the mouth keeps pace with the words rather than chasing them.

  5. 05

    True-to-life movement

    The model returns body language alongside its sentence. Each verb resolves to a clip in that avatar’s own motion library, so the figure is never standing still while it talks.

  6. 06

    AI-powered back end

    Bring your own model, hosted where you choose. The avatar is the face; the intelligence behind it is yours to run.
